## Text

On an empty spot of the page, type `"` to start a text region, or just type a word and a space: a word followed by a space becomes text, and the typing carries on there. Press **Escape** (or click elsewhere) to finish; click the text again to change it.

Text is written in Markdown, and drawn formatted when you leave it:

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `# Heading`, `## Smaller heading` | Headings |
| `**bold**`, `*italic*` | **bold**, *italic* |
| `- item` | A list |
| `[a link](https://beermat.pub)` | A link |

Math inside text isn't worked out: put calculations in math regions beside the text.

## Pictures

**Insert → Picture…** puts a PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or SVG at the crosshair, or paste a picture copied from another program with **Ctrl+V**.

- A picture is kept inside the `.bbm` file, so the sheet stays one file to send.
- Click a picture to select it; drag it to move it, or drag the handle at its bottom-right corner to resize it (it keeps its shape unless you hold **Shift**). **Delete** removes it.
- Right-click it for **Copy as image**.

Pictures over 3 MB make the file large, so Beermat asks first.
