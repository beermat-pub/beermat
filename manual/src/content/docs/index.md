Beermat is an engineering worksheet: you write math on a page the way you would on paper, with real units, and it works out the results.

```
L := 6 m
q := 12 kN/m
M := q·L²/8 = 54 kN·m
```

:::note[More examples]
The examples that come with Beermat (in the browser: **Examples**) show more of what it can do. Something missing or unclear? [Tell us](https://github.com/beermat-pub/beermat/issues).
:::
