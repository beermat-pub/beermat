Beermat is an engineering worksheet: you write math on a page the way you would on paper, with real units, and it works out the results.

```
L := 6 m
q := 12 kN/m
M := q·L²/8 = 54 kN·m
```

:::note[This manual is being written]
This is a preview of Beermat, and its manual is young: more pages arrive with each version. Until then, the examples that come with Beermat (in the browser: **Examples**) show most of what it can do.
:::
