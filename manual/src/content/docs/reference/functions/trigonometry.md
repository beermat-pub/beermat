<a id="fn-sin"></a>

## `sin(x)`

The sine of x, an angle in radians, or with a unit such as deg.

```text
sin(30 deg) = 0.5
```

<a id="fn-cos"></a>

## `cos(x)`

The cosine of x.

```text
cos(60 deg) = 0.5
```

<a id="fn-tan"></a>

## `tan(x)`

The tangent of x.

```text
tan(45 deg) = 1
```

<a id="fn-sec"></a>

## `sec(x)`

The secant of x, 1/cos(x).

```text
sec(π/3) = 2
```

<a id="fn-csc"></a>

## `csc(x)`

The cosecant of x, 1/sin(x).

```text
csc(30 deg) = 2
```

<a id="fn-cot"></a>

## `cot(x)`

The cotangent of x, 1/tan(x).

```text
cot(45 deg) = 1
```

<a id="fn-asin"></a>

## `asin(x)`

The angle whose sine is x, between −π/2 and π/2.

```text
asin(0.5) = 30 deg
```

<a id="fn-acos"></a>

## `acos(x)`

The angle whose cosine is x, between 0 and π.

```text
acos(0.5) = 60 deg
```

<a id="fn-atan"></a>

## `atan(x)`

The angle whose tangent is x, between −π/2 and π/2.

```text
atan(1) = 45 deg
```

<a id="fn-atan2"></a>

## `atan2(x, y)`

The angle of the point (x, y) from the x axis, between −π and π; x and y may have units, the same.

```text
atan2(-1 m, 1 m) = 135 deg
```

<a id="fn-acot"></a>

## `acot(x)`

The angle whose cotangent is x, between 0 and π.

```text
acot(1) = 45 deg
```

<a id="fn-asec"></a>

## `asec(x)`

The angle whose secant is x.

```text
asec(2) = 60 deg
```

<a id="fn-acsc"></a>

## `acsc(x)`

The angle whose cosecant is x.

```text
acsc(2) = 30 deg
```

<a id="fn-sinh"></a>

## `sinh(x)`

The hyperbolic sine of x.

```text
sinh(1) = 1.175
```

<a id="fn-cosh"></a>

## `cosh(x)`

The hyperbolic cosine of x.

```text
cosh(1) = 1.543
```

<a id="fn-tanh"></a>

## `tanh(x)`

The hyperbolic tangent of x.

```text
tanh(1) = 0.762
```

<a id="fn-sech"></a>

## `sech(x)`

The hyperbolic secant of x, 1/cosh(x).

```text
sech(1) = 0.648
```

<a id="fn-csch"></a>

## `csch(x)`

The hyperbolic cosecant of x, 1/sinh(x).

```text
csch(1) = 0.851
```

<a id="fn-coth"></a>

## `coth(x)`

The hyperbolic cotangent of x, 1/tanh(x).

```text
coth(1) = 1.313
```

<a id="fn-asinh"></a>

## `asinh(x)`

The inverse hyperbolic sine of x.

```text
asinh(1) = 0.881
```

<a id="fn-acosh"></a>

## `acosh(x)`

The inverse hyperbolic cosine of x, for x of 1 or more.

```text
acosh(2) = 1.317
```

<a id="fn-atanh"></a>

## `atanh(x)`

The inverse hyperbolic tangent of x, for x between −1 and 1.

```text
atanh(0.5) = 0.549
```

<a id="fn-acoth"></a>

## `acoth(x)`

The inverse hyperbolic cotangent of x, for x beyond −1 and 1.

```text
acoth(2) = 0.549
```
