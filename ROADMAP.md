# Roadmap

What's coming to Beermat, roughly in order. Plans change; [issues](https://github.com/beermat-pub/beermat/issues) and ideas are welcome. What each version brought is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## 1.0, the first public version

Today's previews already have the worksheet, the editor, units, plots, symbolic algebra, printing and PDF, `.xmcd` import, the command line, AI assistants through MCP, and the web version. Before 1.0:

- **The manual** at [beermat.pub/docs](https://beermat.pub/docs/): getting started, tutorials, how-to guides, and a reference of every function, unit and keystroke, with examples drawn by Beermat itself. F1 on a function opens its page.
- **Spreadsheet components** from `.xmcd` files whose formulas run, not only their last values.
- **Python packages of your own** for Python functions in a sheet.
- **Short videos** showing how it works.

## After 1.0

- **A VS Code extension**: `.bbm` files open on the page, as in the app, with the text one click away.
- **Export to Word**: text, editable Word equations, results, plots and pictures.
- **Math in table cells** as you type, and **math inside text regions**.
- **More functions**: the named curve fits (`genfit`, `expfit`…) and `loess`; boundary-value and stiff differential equations.
- **Polar plots.**
- **Copy a selection of regions as an image.**
- **Importing other worksheet formats.**
- **The manual inside the app**, for working offline.
- **Signed installers.**

## Later

- Contour and 3D plots.
- Locked areas, and referencing another worksheet.
- Controls on the page (check boxes, lists, sliders) that change a value.
- Translations, and the decimal comma.
